Request Account Deletion

If you no longer wish to use Liquor Stock Manager, you can request permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data. You do not need to reinstall or access the mobile app to submit a deletion request.

How to Submit Your Request

1
Send an email to craftxdesignlabs@gmail.com
2
Use the subject line: LSM – Account Deletion Request
3
Include the email address registered with your Liquor Stock Manager account
4
Our support team may contact you to verify your account ownership before processing
5
Once verified, your deletion request will be processed within 30 days
✉ Send Deletion Request

What Data Will Be Deleted?

⚠ This action is permanent. Once your account is deleted, your data cannot be recovered.

Following verification, we will delete your account and associated data, subject to applicable legal and regulatory requirements. This includes:

  • Account and profile information
  • Authentication credentials and session data
  • Store membership and staff role assignments
  • Business records associated with your account (sales, expenses, invoices, stock movements, closing reports) where deletion is permitted

Data Retention

Certain records may need to be retained where required by applicable laws, regulatory obligations, security requirements, or legitimate fraud-prevention purposes. Any such retained information will be handled according to our Privacy Policy and applicable retention requirements.

Processing Time

We will review and verify your request before processing deletion. We will communicate the expected completion timeframe and notify you when the request has been completed. Most requests are processed within 30 days of verification.
